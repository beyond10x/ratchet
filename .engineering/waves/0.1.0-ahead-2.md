# Wave ahead 2 of the waves to 0.1.0

Plan: [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) § "Beside the waves". Run by the skill `aep:implementing`,
wave mode. Approval: the owner's choice D87 B of 2026-11-26; no `approval-record` holds it yet.

## Why the wave exists

The owner chose on 2026-11-26 (D87 B) a second wave ahead of 0.1.0: the parts of the format and
the specification that need no command, the seven drafts filed from the gaps of a third format
consumer, each split into a part for now and a part in `ratchet`, and bigger waves: more
stories and gap closures in one pull request. Every planned wave still starts behind
`story:cli-skeleton-validate`, which waits for an ESS release (beyond10x/ess#836), and every
command of the `ratchet` binary answers `cli_handler_unavailable`.

So the 33 stories of this wave change only `docs/design.md`, `docs/evidence-format.md`,
`spec/domains/*.yaml`, `presets/`, `examples/`, the POSIX sh checks under `spec/` and
`.github/scripts/`, `README` files and `CHANGELOG.md`, and `crates/ratchet-cli-contract/` where
`task generate` rewrites it after a change of `spec/domains/cli.yaml`. Each acceptance item is
observed through the steps of `task check`, those scripts, `ess specify validate`, or files.
Each story takes the reversible answer to the form it decides (`AGENTS.md` § "Phase:
implementation") and names it in its body.

Goal first: the wave moves the gaps of the three format consumers (`beyond10x/inference`,
`beyond10x/swell`, and the consumer of the seven drafts) and of the catalog's own pages
("Not expressible yet"); it builds nothing the first consumer's UI loop waits for, which needs
the command.
