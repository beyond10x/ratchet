# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
A release is the workspace version at a bare tag such as `0.1.0`, with Linux archives of the `ratchet` binary and their checksums.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- A record of results kept as a file of the tree, such as the results of a run made by hand, names the import source `tree_file` with its path relative to the root and holds `target.revision`, the commit the file was read at: `docs/evidence-format.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare it, with § 6 example 7; no command imports a record yet
- An output declares the conditions its results are split by as `cells`, such as a viewport, and a result carries its condition `cell` beside its scope item, so a scenario walked in two viewports is two results and not one item named `<scenario>/<viewport>`; a measurement may read one cell: `docs/design.md` § 3, `docs/evidence-format.md` § 3 and § 10 and `spec/` declare it, with § 6 example 8; no command reads a cell yet
- An assessor learns the keys of the plan's observation, `scenarios`, `workload`, `command`, `runs` and `min_samples`, as inputs it maps under `observation_inputs` of an assessor declaration, and a result over the runs of a plan carries their number as `runs`: `docs/evidence-format.md` § 10 and `spec/` declare it, `presets/timing-report@2` and `event-time@2` use it, and `task check:spec` refuses a plan that passes such an input under `inputs` too or lacks the key where the input is required, and a declaration that maps an unknown key or an input it does not declare; no command starts an assessor yet
- A time-window plan over a population outside the record store passes its window through the observation keys `window_length`, `window_last` and `window_ends_at`, and each field of its population filter as `--<field>`: `docs/evidence-format.md` § 10 and `spec/` declare them, with two YAML examples, and `task check:spec` reads `window_length` and `window_last` at the `length` and the `last` of `observation.window`, refuses `window_ends_at` as a required input of a plan that is no time-window plan, and refuses an input of a declaration that has the name of one of its `population_fields`; no command starts an assessor yet
- An assessor of a `build` target names the input that holds the artifact as `target_input`, is passed `--revision`, the revision the artifact was built from, and writes the digest of the artifact as `candidate`: `docs/evidence-format.md` § 10 and `spec/` declare it, with § 6 example 9, and `presets/` holds the declaration `file-size@2` and the preset `binary-size@2` over a `build` target; that the artifact was built from that revision is the project's statement, which nothing checks, and `binary-size@1` stays as released
- An imported report states the revision it was produced from where its input is named under `revision_inputs` of an assessor declaration, and the record carries it as `inputs.<input>.revision`: `docs/evidence-format.md` § 10 and `spec/` declare it, `presets/conformance-report@2` names its report there, and `task check:spec` refuses an entry that names no input of the declaration; `ratchet assess`, which refuses a report of another revision, is not built
- `docs/evidence-format.md` § 12 defines the proof record `ratchet-proof/1`, the planted defects of a requirement run at a revision and the ids refused and accepted, with its field table and an example that `task check:spec` decodes against `spec/`; that no id stands twice is a rule a reader checks and not an invariant of the specification, and `ratchet prove` is not built
- In a requirement, a condition compares a `digest` with `eq`, such as the digest of a generated file, and a `digest` stands on no other operator and no other value type: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare it, and `task check:spec` refuses another form in a preset; no command evaluates a condition yet
- A requirement states a range with the operator `within` with `min` and `max`, both inclusive, on a number or the number of items of a set, and the limit row of the gate report carries both bounds: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare it, and `task check:spec` refuses a range without a bound, with `min` above `max`, with a `value`, on an assertion, or a bound on another operator; no command evaluates a condition yet
- A requirement compares two measurements: `against` names the second, `lt`, `lte`, `gt`, `gte` and `eq` compare two numbers of one unit as they are, with no factor and no tolerance, and the operator `subset_of` with `against` holds when every item of the measured set is in the other set; `docs/design.md` § 3 and § 5 and `spec/` declare it with its validation codes and the cause of a `subset_of` that does not hold, `task check:spec` refuses another value type or unit on the two sides, and `presets/` holds `known-failures@2`, whose plan over the suite report is named `known-failures` and whose plan over the list is named `list`, and `conformance-report@2` with the output `failing_cases`; within a preset `against` names a plan of the same preset, and no command evaluates a condition yet
- A limit and a tolerance may state where their number comes from, the `source` of a number, `NumberSource` (`cites` and `since`), which the gate report prints beside the allowed value and which judges nothing: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare it
- A measurement may read a count of the coverage of its run through the output name `coverage.<count>`, which Ratchet reserves, so a reference can protect the number of executed cases: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare it, and `task check:spec` refuses such a measurement that is not exact, not at the scope `run`, or has a unit, and a count the declaration does not list under `coverage`; no command evaluates it yet
- A plan bounds the executed units whose own outcome is a fail with the coverage bound `failed` in `operation-time@2`, from `CoverageBounds.failed` that `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare; `operation-time@1` stays as released
- A measurement of an assertion names the items it must have with `min_items` and `required_items` on a measurement, and a result below either is insufficient evidence with the cause `coverage_below_plan`: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare them, and `presets/health-readiness@2` states `min_items: 1` on each endpoint; no command evaluates them yet
- The `meanings` of a measurement give each item of a list a statement shown beside its result, and the rows of `ratchet explain` and of the gate report carry `meanings` and the meanings without a result: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare them, and `task check:spec` refuses a statement that is a number; neither command is built
- A measurement at a scope other than `run` bounds the samples behind the value of each item with `min_samples_per_item` on a measurement, and an item below it is insufficient evidence for that item alone: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare it and make it a validation error at the scope `run`; no command evaluates it yet
- A plan declares the machine and the build it qualifies under as the conditions `cpu_model`, `memory`, `accelerator` and `build_profile`, beside `host_load_per_cpu`, `tools` and `runner`, and a record observes them: `docs/design.md` § 3, `docs/evidence-format.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare them, and `task check:spec` accepts them in a preset without a declaration that lists them; no command reads a condition yet
- A preset passes the conditions of a plan from its binding through an input of the input type `conditions`, the whole value of `observation.conditions`, such as a bound on the host load for the class `local` and a runner for the class `ci`: `docs/design.md` § 4 and `spec/` declare it, `presets/operation-time@2` takes it in place of a fixed `runner`, and the other presets of the latency, resources, start, build, artifact and code families still fix their conditions; no command resolves a binding yet
- `presets/` holds `code-lines@2` with the allowed `values` of an input, `[lower, higher]` for the direction of each count: `InputDeclaration.values` of `docs/design.md` § 4 and `spec/` lists the words an input of the type `string` may have, and `task check:spec` refuses a default, a list default or a literal that a plan passes that is none of them; `code-lines@1` stays as released, and nothing checks the value a binding passes until a command resolves a binding
- A plan names what its command needs before it runs, environment variables and files by name and whether a run is billed, as `needs` of an observation plan, and a run on a machine that lacks a need does not start: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` declare it; no command starts a run yet
- A plan that is never run for a revision says so with a reason as `no_run_expected` of an assessment plan, and `ratchet status` tells a plan assessed, never assessed and with no run expected apart: `docs/design.md` § 5 and `spec/` declare it; `ratchet status` is not built
- A reference with a value per scope item says how an item it holds no value for is judged with `new_items` of a reference: `insufficient_evidence`, or `not_judged`, which leaves the item out of the verdict and names it under `items_not_judged` of the requirement's verdict, while a requirement whose items are all new stays `reference_missing`: `docs/design.md` § 5 and § 6 and `spec/` declare it; no command evaluates a reference yet
- An accepted value may be adopted from another plan of the same specification with `{from: assessment, assessment: <plan>, measurement: <measurement>}`, such as the set of cases a reference implementation passes, and the preset `differential-equality@3` adopts from another assessment: its `no-reference-case-lost` takes its accepted set from the plan `reference-run`; `task check:spec` refuses such an adoption whose measurement has another value type or unit, and `assessment` or `measurement` on another `from`, and fails when `docs/design.md` or a page of `presets/` still calls this adoption or `against` inexpressible; `differential-equality@1` and `@2` stay as released, and no command adopts a reference yet
- `[records] tracked` of `ratchet.toml` names directories of the tree that hold committed evidence records, such as the baseline records a clone needs; a command reads them beside the local record directory and the ref and writes none there: `docs/design.md` § 5 and `spec/` declare it; no command reads records yet
- `when: {not_only: [...]}` on a gate entry skips the entry when every changed path is in the list, the negative form of `when: {changed: [...]}`: `docs/design.md` § 5 and `spec/` declare it; `ratchet evaluate` is not built
- A candidate gate may judge the commit of a tag with `judges: {tag: <pattern>}` on a gate, and for another candidate `ratchet evaluate` writes no decision and returns the refusal `candidate_not_tagged`, declared in the binding `spec/cli.yaml`: `docs/design.md` § 5 and `spec/` declare it; `ratchet evaluate` is not built
- A production gate is a candidate gate with `judges: {deployment: <class>}` on a gate, and `examples/production-gate.ratchet.yaml` uses it: `docs/design.md` § 5 and `spec/` declare it, and until the design says which revision is deployed in a class `ratchet evaluate` judges the revision given with `--candidate`; there is no third gate kind, and `ratchet evaluate` is not built
- A requirement over a `document` plan joins the candidate revision by the digest of the document's file at that revision, and the verdict names the document it joined by path and digest, `ratchet.gate.JoinedDocument` on a verdict and `document` on the row of the gate report: `docs/design.md` § 5 and § 6 and `spec/` declare it; the generated CLI contract carries `document` in its `binding.json` and names no `JoinedDocument` type, and `ratchet evaluate` is not built
- `examples/release-quality.ratchet.yaml`, a gate of only report entries over three exact counts of a release, with the declaration `release-counts@1` it names; no program of that assessor ships, and no command evaluates the example yet

### Changed

- A reader of the specification finds each cited rule again: the table of `spec/README.md` cites `docs/design.md` by heading and phrase in place of line numbers, so an insertion into the design no longer moves a citation out of step
- The six coverage counts of an evidence record are optional: an absent count is not known and a written 0 stays 0, and a coverage count names a lower bound under `at_least`, such as executed cases that a run cut short counted only in part; `docs/evidence-format.md` § 3, `spec/` and the coverage row of the CLI contract follow, with § 6 example 6
- In the production example, check 6 of `examples/production-gate.ratchet.yaml` is `no-critical-advisory`, which limits critical advisories with `eq 0` on a filtered identity set and no reference; no new form, and no command evaluates the example yet
- `docs/design.md` § 3 states that an aggregation of one value is that value, so a preset declares its aggregation also for `runs: 1` and a result of one run carries `samples: 1` and is judged as observed; no new form, and the presets of the build, latency, resources and start families stay as they are
- `docs/design.md` § 2 states that a comparison of implementations is outside the boundary of Ratchet: a consumer that wants values of other implementations beside the candidate shows values of other implementations from records it keeps itself, and Ratchet neither stores nor shows them

## [0.0.4] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- A release holds a third archive, `ratchet-<version>-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl.tar.gz`, a static `ratchet` that runs on a Linux host with any C library or none, such as one whose glibc is older than the build runner's: `.github/scripts/static-check.sh` refuses a binary with a `NEEDED` entry or an `INTERP` program header, `task check:static` builds the musl binary and runs it with an empty environment, the forge runs it in an Alpine image pinned by digest, and `release-verify.sh` checks it in a published release; there is no musl archive for `aarch64`, and the two glibc archives stay

### Changed

- `task check` runs the `ess` release `spec/ess-inputs.yaml` requires also on a host whose `ess` in `PATH` is newer and holds that release beside it: `ESS_TOOLCHAIN` in `Taskfile.yml` pins it to the release the forge installs, and `task check:scripts` fails when the pin differs from the forge's or from the requirement, or when a call of `ess` in `task check:spec` or `task check:generated` loses it; `.github/scripts/check-generated.sh` started directly, not through `task`, gets no pin
- `docs/evidence-format.md` § 3 and `spec/domains/evidence.yaml` name the identifiers of a target of the kind `workflow_run`, `run_id` and `job` (`<workflow> / <job>`), and its candidate, the commit id the run ran at, with a record of such a run in § 6; `identifiers` stays an open map, and nothing refuses a record without these names or with others
- `docs/design.md` § 3, `docs/evidence-format.md` § 3 and `spec/` state that a record under `selection: merge_result` holds the revision under judgement as `candidate` and the merged tree that was read as `target.revision`, with a record of such a run in § 6; nothing refuses a record with the two swapped yet, and the assessor invocation of § 10 still passes one revision
- The cache condition has one name, `cache_profile`, in a plan, an assessor declaration and an evidence record: `docs/design.md`, `docs/evidence-format.md` and `spec/` say so, the design example writes it, and the example declaration `command-timing@1` (`examples/quality/assessors/command-timing@1.assessor.yaml`) now declares `cache_profile` under `conditions`, changed in place: an example declaration is an illustration that no plan binds by its digest; and `task check:spec` refuses a condition `cache` in a preset or in the `conditions` of a declaration of `presets/`; `ratchet validate` does not refuse it yet
- `docs/`, `spec/` and the README of the specification answer five places the first consumer of the formats could not read: `scopes: []` is valid and means the scope `run` alone, and `scopes` stays required; with `runs: 1` an existing aggregation kind is valid where the command repeats its measurement itself in the one run; an output has one unit and every value of it is written in that unit; a `ratchet/1` file may hold comments, which a reader skips; `docs/evidence-format.md` § 2 gives the recipe for the evidence digest, § 6 states that of its first example, and `task check:spec` fails when the two disagree
- `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/domains/specification.yaml` state that one plan may hold exact and sampled measurements of its one run, in every observation kind, and that the conditions of the plan qualify every measurement of it, its exact ones included; no form of the mix is refused, and the header of `spec/presets.test.sh` says so
- `task check:spec` refuses an exact measurement of a preset that aggregates with anything but `sum` and an input of an assessor declaration named as a reserved argument, such as `out`; the header of `spec/presets.test.sh` lists under "Not checked", each with its reason, the twelve other forms it accepts without a word, and a case of its harness fails the run when the comparison of the line printed with `--catalog` and `--spec` is taken out
- A measurement may state `over_all_runs: true`, a value its assessor computes over all runs of the plan and no aggregation, such as a flake rate: `docs/design.md` § 3 and `spec/` say that a sampled measurement of a plan with `runs` above 1 states an aggregation or that member, and `task check:spec` refuses a preset whose plan has a literal `runs` above 1 and whose sampled measurement states neither, or states both; `presets/flake-rate@2` states it, `flake-rate@1` stays as released, and a `runs` that an input gives is not read, so no check reaches a binding yet
- An output of an assessor declaration may list the closed values of an item field under `item_values`, such as `{class: [equal, allowed, different]}`: `docs/evidence-format.md` § 10 and `spec/` declare it, `task check:spec` refuses a `filter` of a preset that names another value of such a field and a key of `item_values` that is no field of `item_fields`, and a field without a list stays open; `presets/comparison-report@2` lists the three classes and `differential-equality@2` names it, the files `@1` stay as released, and neither `ratchet validate` nor an evidence reader checks the values yet
- `presets/` holds a ninth family, production readiness (`presets/production.md`): `exposure-probes@1`, three probe assertions of a deployed service (requests without a valid credential refused, console routes unreachable through the public proxy, TLS on every public listener), and `artifact-signatures@1`, the signatures of an image and its provenance, with the declarations `http-probe-report@1` and `artifact-verification-report@1`; `examples/production-gate.ratchet.yaml` is a candidate gate over seven production checks that binds them beside `conformance-cases@1` and `known-failures@1`, and `task check:spec` refuses a binding of an example whose `use:` names no preset file of `presets/` or whose `digest` is not that file's SHA-256; the catalog ships no probe runner, no verifier and no program that reads their reports, no release judges an assertion yet, and nothing checks the inputs of a binding

## [0.0.2] - 2026-11-25

### Added

- `presets/` holds the preset catalog with its first family: the presets `conformance-cases@1`, `differential-equality@1` and `known-failures@1` and the assessor declarations they name; the catalog ships no program that reads a report
- `presets/functional.md` states for each measurement of the functional family the direction, the unit, the tolerance and the source of the evidence, and names what `ratchet/1` cannot say yet
- A preset is the file `<name>@<version>.preset.yaml` in a catalog source and holds `statements`, `assessments` and `requirements`; no command resolves a binding yet, and `presets/README.md` says what a project writes until one does
- `task check:spec` checks every file of `presets/` against the specification, each preset against the declaration of its assessor and against the comparisons the design allows for the value type of an output, and every measurement against its row on a page of the catalog
- A reference to an input has the one spelling `${inputs.<name>}`: a value of a preset that holds `${` or `{inputs.` in another form is an error and no text
- `presets/` holds the presets of four more families, observed while a project's own workload runs against a started service: `operation-time@1`, `event-time@1` and `workload-throughput@1` (latency and throughput), `process-resources@1` (memory, CPU, file descriptors, threads), `start-time@1` (start to ready, ready to first answer), and `metric-names@1` and `health-readiness@1` (operability), with the five assessor declarations they name; the catalog ships no workload, no load runner and no program that reads a report
- `presets/latency.md`, `presets/resources.md`, `presets/start.md` and `presets/operability.md` state for each measurement the direction, the unit, the tolerance input and the source of the evidence, and name what `ratchet/1` and `ratchet-assessor/1` cannot say yet
- Every duration, memory value, rate and count of a running service in these presets is sampled: its tolerance, the number of runs, the runs of an adoption and the workload are required inputs without a default, and no release judges a sampled value yet; of the four families 0.1.0 judges the metric names of `metric-names@1` alone
- `task check:spec` refuses a preset whose plan of a sampled measurement declares no condition (a window over `runs_of` declares none), and a requirement that compares a sampled measurement with an accepted value and has no tolerance or no `adoption`
- `presets/` holds the presets of three more families, judged from reports a project's own build, test, package and lint tools write: `cold-build-time@1`, `warm-build-time@1`, `test-step-time@1`, `test-time@1` and `flake-rate@1` (build), `image-size@1`, `binary-size@1`, `dependency-count@1` and `dependency-advisories@1` (artifact), and `code-lines@1`, `generator-obligations@1` and `lint-findings@1` (code), with the nine assessor declarations they name; the catalog ships no build tool, no test runner, no scanner, no linter and no program that reads a report
- `presets/build.md`, `presets/artifact.md` and `presets/code.md` state for each measurement the direction, the unit, the tolerance and the source of the evidence, define a cold and a warm build, a flaky test, the size of an image, a dependency and the identity of a lint finding, and name what `ratchet/1` and `ratchet-assessor/1` cannot say yet
- The five measurements of the build family are sampled: the tolerance, the number of runs and the command are required inputs without a default, the limits of the test step and of its slowest test are inputs with the defaults 120 s and 60 s, and no release judges them yet; the ten measurements of the artifact and the code family are exact numbers and sets, which 0.1.0 judges, and no command resolves a binding yet

### Changed

- CI runs `task check` for every change under `presets/`, a page included, and for a file renamed out of `presets/` or `crates/`
- The site says what release 0.0.1 ships in place of "nothing is implemented": a `ratchet` binary that parses the 16 commands and prints its version; no command is built yet, so it checks nothing for a project

## [0.0.1] - 2026-11-25

### Added

- The specification in `spec/` declares the four file formats: `ratchet/1`, `ratchet-evidence/1`, `ratchet-assessor/1` and `ratchet-reference/1`
- `spec/cli.yaml` declares the 16 `ratchet` commands with the typed inputs, results and refusals of each
- A coverage bound is written `{min: N}` or `{max: N}` and an adoption `{from: ...}`; the format has no shorter spelling of them
- `docs/evidence-format.md` describes what an assessor writes (`ratchet-evidence/1`) and how it declares itself (`ratchet-assessor/1`)
- A `ratchet` binary that parses the 16 commands and prints their help; no command is built yet, so it reads no file and evaluates nothing
- Each of the 16 commands answers exit status 1 with `cli_handler_unavailable` on standard error until a release builds it
- `ratchet --version` prints `ratchet <version>`, the version of the workspace, and exits 0
- The parser, the help text and a bash completion are generated from `spec/` into `crates/ratchet-cli-contract`, and the gate fails when they differ from the specification
- `task check` is the gate of the repository: the specification, format and lints, the generated crate, the script tests, the tests and the site
- The tests have a budget: a test is ended at 60 s and the run at 120 s, each as a failure
- The compiler is pinned in `rust-toolchain.toml`, and CI runs `task check` with it on every change of code
- A tag `<major>.<minor>.<patch>` builds `ratchet-<version>-<target>.tar.gz` for `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu` and `aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu` with `SHA256SUMS`
- `.github/scripts/release-verify.sh <version>` checks a published release: its author, its three assets, their checksums and the version the binary prints
- `AGENTS.md` § "Release" holds the steps of a release and the rule for the lines of this file
